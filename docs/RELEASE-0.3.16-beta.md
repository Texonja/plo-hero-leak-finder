# 0.3.16 beta

Open **Reports → Luck and variance** to compare your PLO4 deals and runouts with exact card probabilities. Filter by room, big blind, date or Hero alias. Select a row to read its definition, or turn on the z-score chart. Parser warnings link to example replays.

The first visit builds a local index. Subsequent imports update the affected hands and the totals for the active filters. The report works in HeroDemo and personal libraries.

- **Review by date.** Choose **Hands → By date** to review decisions from a specific day.
- **Action critiques.** Added reviews for selected squeezes, limp-reraises and costly heads-up flop calls. The cards show the stacks still in play at the decision.

On Windows, extract the ZIP, open the **PLO Hero Leak Finder** folder and double-click **START.BAT**.
