The detector and strength snapshots did not include a top-level license.

the cited MIT component. Sample derivation details are in demo/README.txt.

Preflop reference

Small-blind first-in fold frequencies are derived from the PokerData PLO4
six-max 100 BB sample (5% rake, capped at 1 BB).
https://pokerdata.io/samples/plo6-6max-100bb-sample.zip

Windows runtime

CPython for Windows x64, Tcl/Tk, SQLite and standard runtime components
are included under runtime/. See runtime/LICENSE.txt and component notices
shipped with those files. The Windows GUI launcher uses distlib. Its
license is included in runtime/distlib-LICENSE.txt.
