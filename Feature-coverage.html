Reviewing an import

Hand review selects up to six distinct Hero hands from the latest import or the full library. Each entry identifies the decision, gives a reason to review it, and describes the available comparison. Supported frequency differences appear first. Exploratory examples also cover decisions with few matching observations. Room and street filters change the selection.

Open table shows the original decision before Hero acts, including dealt cards where available, public cards through that street, stacks, pot, price to call, and earlier actions. The full recorded history and its highlighted source line or XML action are available from the table.

Implemented coverage

The desktop contains 42 defined metrics and result measures. Fourteen decision nodes have local, context-matched population comparisons. The three-bet versus a single open and the in-position flop stab are eligible for supported findings. Other nodes remain available in exploratory or descriptive reports. Every row opens its source hands.

The reporting menus include Position, Stack depth, Stake, Room, Hand class, Chosen sizing, Faced sizing, Opponent, Session, Trends, Day and Month. The desktop includes a separate HeroDemo library and manual Import menu. The recorded references below apply to both networks.

Reading the reports

Size is chips added by an opening bet divided by the pot immediately before that bet. Bands end at 33%, 50%, 75% and 100%. Faced size refers to the opponent’s bet. Stack depth uses each actor’s starting stack for hand measures and effective stack before action for decisions. Class tags overlap. Premium AAxx here means exactly two aces, double suited, with both side cards ten or higher. A four-card rundown uses four distinct consecutive ranks, including A234. Gap counts refer to missing ranks in the shortest four-rank span. Disconnected requires a span of at least eight rank steps and at most one adjacent pair in either ace ordering.

Sessions are inferred from gaps longer than 45 minutes within each network. Temporal comparisons follow recorded timestamps. Direct-opponent reports group identified decision counterparts. Opponent profit is shown only for hands dealt heads-up from the start. Whole-hand realized BB/100 uses reconciled payouts only and has no role in ranking. Showdown wins count positive awards, including split pots.
