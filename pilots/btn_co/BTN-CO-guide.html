Hand guide · BTN vs CO

Open a source hand or select a recommendation to see the PLO4 preflop rule behind it.

Six-handed PLO4 cash. UTG and HJ fold, CO opens to 2–3.5 BB, BTN acts, and both blinds are active. CO and BTN start with 60–150 BB effective after antes. Both blinds have at least 40 BB behind. Any ante is uniform and at most 0.16 BB per player. Rake, squeeze frequency and opponent ranges can change the preferred action within this scope.

Two actions indicate a close decision. A difference from the guide is a reason to review the hand. Strategy and frequencies need independent validation.
